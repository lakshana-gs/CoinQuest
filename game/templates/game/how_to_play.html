How to Play Coin Quest

Everything you need to know about controls, mechanics, hazard evasion, and scoring!

🎮

Keyboard & Touch Controls

A / D or ← → Move Left / Move Right
Space / W or ↑ Jump (Hold for higher jump)
ESC or P Pause or Resume Game
R Instant Level Restart
M Mute / Unmute Audio Synthesizer
On-Screen Buttons Touch controls for mobile or tablets
🪙

Core Objectives

  • Gather Gold Coins: Each realm has a required target of coins (e.g., 8, 12, 14, 16, 20).
  • Awaken the Exit Portal: The portal starts dormant and gray. Once you collect the required coins, it begins spinning with vibrant golden energy!
  • Step Into the Portal: Jump into the active portal to conclude the run and unlock the next realm.
  • Preserve Your Lives: You start with 3 lives. Colliding with spikes or enemies deducts 1 life and gives brief invulnerability. Falling into the bottomless void resets your position to the start and costs 1 life.
⭐

Scoring & Star Ratings

Scores are validated and saved to the Django database upon level completion:

Final Score = (Coins × 100) + (Lives Remaining × 500) + Time Bonus
  • Coin Points: 100 points for every gold coin collected.
  • Survival Bonus: 500 points for each remaining heart (up to 1,500 bonus points).
  • Speed Bonus: Extra points for completing the realm faster than the target Par Time.
  • Star Ratings:
    • ★ 1 Star: Complete the realm with target coins.
    • ★★ 2 Stars: Complete with all target coins and at least 2 lives.
    • ★★★ 3 Stars: Collect 100% of all coins in the realm under the par time!
⚠️

Hazards & Traps

▲ Spikes

Sharp crystalline or metallic thorns on ground or ceilings. Touch them and you take damage!

👾 Patrolling Sentries

Lava slimes and cyber drones patrolling floating platforms back and forth. Time your leap over them!

↔ Moving Platforms

Floating platforms gliding smoothly across bottomless gaps. Ride them to reach elevated ledges.
